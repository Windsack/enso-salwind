## Research question

Is mean wind speed at Amílcar Cabral Airport (GVAC) from December 2026
through February 2027 below the historical neutral DJF reference value?

## Outcome measure and data coverage

The primary outcome is the equally weighted mean of the three monthly
mean wind speeds for December 2026, January 2027 and February 2027,
expressed in knots. The first valid wind report in each UTC hourly slot
is selected using the preprint's method. Each monthly mean is calculated
by first averaging the observations for each of the 24 UTC hours of
the day across the month and then weighting those 24 means equally.

A month is eligible if observations cover at least 80% of all UTC hourly
slots and, for each UTC hour, a valid report is available on at least
70% of the month's days. If any of the three months fails either
criterion, the primary winter comparison remains unresolved. Eligible
monthly values are reported individually.

## Reference and decision rule

The fixed reference is **13.55 kt**, the neutral DJF comparison value
in Table 1 of the preprint. It is based on ten neutral months from six
winters. The historical means for December, January and February were
weighted equally.

If the unrounded winter value is below 13.55 kt, the result agrees with
the directional expectation. At 13.55 kt or above, the expectation is
not met for this season. The winter value, its difference from the
reference and the data coverage of all three months are reported.

The median of the two fully neutral winters in Figure S1 is
**13.80 kt** and provides additional context.

## ENSO classification

The ENSO classification is reported separately for December, January
and February using the preprint's NOAA RONI rule. The RONI data version
used is documented. Classification as a complete El Niño winter
requires all three months to belong to the same El Niño episode under
that rule. A mixed or unresolved classification is labelled accordingly
in the wind comparison.

The comparison describes one additional season at GVAC.
