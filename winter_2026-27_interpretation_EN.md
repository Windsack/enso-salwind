# GVAC · Interpretation of the winter 2026/27 comparison

Matthias Baier · Original dated 17 November 2026

English translation prepared on 18 November 2026.
[German original](winter_2026-27_einordnung.md), published in
[release winter-plan-2026-27-v1](https://github.com/Windsack/enso-salwind/releases/tag/winter-plan-2026-27-v1).

Companion to the [analysis plan](gvac_analysis_plan_winter_2026-27_EN.md).

If all three months meet the data coverage requirements, the DJF mean
wind speed at Amílcar Cabral Airport (GVAC) and its difference from the
fixed neutral reference of 13.55 kt are reported. A value below that
reference agrees with the expected direction for the 2026/27 season.
At 13.55 kt or above, the directional expectation is not supported
for this season. If any month fails the coverage requirements, the
primary comparison remains unresolved.

The two fully neutral winters in Figure S1 had mean wind speeds of
13.26 and 14.34 kt. They provide descriptive context. The comparison
describes one additional season at GVAC. The ENSO classification is
reported according to the analysis plan.
